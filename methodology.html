Research standards

Evidence over labels.

Our proposed risk methodology is built around explicit checks, traceable evidence, and visible limitations.

Planned assessment model

What we examine.

These are planned modules, not live security checks. Only public market lookup is functional in the current prototype.

01

Source verification

Determine whether verified source code and implementation details can be inspected.

02

Control and permissions

Review owner privileges, upgradeability, mint authority, pauses, blacklists, and other sensitive operations where detectable.

03

Token concentration

Evaluate distribution of token balances while distinguishing known contracts and liquidity pools when possible.

04

Liquidity context

Show indexed liquidity and trading activity as context, not as proof of safety.

05

Explainable classification

Planned Low, Medium, and High risk categories will require documented evidence and tested detection rules. Insufficient Data will be shown when evidence is inadequate.

No automated security rating or audit is currently offered. A future Low Risk label would never guarantee that a contract is safe.
